# Review notes

Feedback on each version of the video. When Jeet asks for a rebuild, apply the open items below, then add Claude's own creative improvements on top.

## v1 review (Jeet, after the first full render)

**Overall:** the words are perfect (no major changes), the animation is almost perfect, and the background music is right. The notes below are polish.

### To change on the next rebuild

1. **Outro copy must not sound like job-hunting.**
   "Please hire my human." / "I need premium cat food." is funny, but it reads as if Jeet is begging for a job. Keep the cat's humour; make it confident and proud, not asking.

2. **Perfectionist scene: replace the "Hire me" button.**
   Same reason as 1. Use a different element for the 1px-nudge gag.

3. **Pacing: give the text time to be read.**
   The speed and energy are good, but text sometimes leaves the screen while it's still being read. Hold lines longer or reduce what's on screen at once, so every line can be read comfortably.

4. **Sound: add the part that touches the heart.**
   The mix is "almost perfect, not the best". The background groove is right, but it's missing a moment that is truly emotional. Add a melodic or harmonic moment that lands emotionally.

5. **Premium sound effects where the eye expects them.**
   - The cards dropping in "What I actually do" need a satisfying, clearly audible impact.
   - The screen moving between sections (transitions) needs a premium whoosh or swipe.
   - The mouse click and nudges in the perfectionist scene need a crisp, audible click.

   Note: v1 does have a thock, whoosh and click at these moments, but they are too quiet or thin to register. Make them premium and audible, not just present.

6. **Animation: more attention to detail and creativity.**
   v1 is "also perfect", but Jeet invites more craft and creative ideas.

7. **Major: drop the website header in the hero scene.**
   The nav bar (logo, Home/Services/About…, social buttons) makes the scene look like a screenshot of the website. This is a video, so the hero should feel like a cinematic scene in the portfolio's style, not a webpage.

8. **Length is flexible.**
   To fix the pacing in 3, the video may run longer than 30 s. Claude decides the new length.

### Keep as is
- All wording outside the two items above.
- The background music style (qawwali and classical fusion).
- The portfolio look: colours, fonts, doodles, the cat as host.
